Oslr: the Doximity Talent Finder alternative for clinical hiring

Doximity is the largest physician network, and Talent Finder lets recruiters reach its members through job posts and DocMail. Oslr is a search tool you run yourself, across every clinical role.

Why recruiters choose Oslr

Choose Oslr if you recruit across clinical roles, want contact details in your own tools, or need to start without a sales cycle.

  • Every clinical role in one search, including nurses, CRNAs and therapists
  • Training year read from start dates for resident and fellow pipelines

Where Doximity Talent Finder fits

Doximity Talent Finder is a good choice if physician messaging inside Doximity is your main channel, or you want placement services.

  • The largest physician network, used daily by physicians
  • Messages land where physicians already are
  • Placement and locum services if you want help filling the role
  • Useful market resources like a compensation report and vacancy cost calculator
