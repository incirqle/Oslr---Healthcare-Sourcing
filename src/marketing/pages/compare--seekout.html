Oslr: the SeekOut alternative for clinical hiring

SeekOut is an agentic recruiting platform for enterprise teams across industries, with a healthcare offering strongest in nursing. Oslr is built only for clinical hiring, with physician training stage at its core.

Why recruiters choose Oslr

Choose Oslr if physician and resident pipelines are your core, or you recruit into private practices.

  • Physician training stage from real start dates
  • Subspecialty depth for surgical and procedural specialties
  • Clinical team intel for private practices
  • A focused tool with a short learning curve

Where SeekOut fits

SeekOut is a good choice if nurse licensure is a hard requirement, or you want screening agents across many job families.

  • Large healthcare dataset with a strong nursing focus
  • Nurse license data across many states
  • Agents for sourcing, screening and applicant review
  • Mature trust program with a dated bias audit
