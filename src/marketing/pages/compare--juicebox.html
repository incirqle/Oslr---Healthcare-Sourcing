Why recruiters choose Oslr

Choose Oslr if your roles are clinical, training stage matters, or you recruit into private practices.

  • Healthcare only, so results never include non-clinical staff
  • Reads the training year from start dates, so PGY-5 means fifth year now
  • Separates subspecialties like sports medicine, joint reconstruction and spine
  • Clinical team intel for private practices and small groups
  • Pricing built around how healthcare recruiters reveal contacts

Where Juicebox fits

Juicebox is a good choice if you also hire outside healthcare, or want outreach and agents in the same tool.

  • Covers every industry, so one tool serves non-clinical roles too
  • Always-on sourcing agents and built-in outreach
  • A free plan to start
  • SOC 2 Type II, a trust center and an AI audit center
  • 40+ ATS integrations
