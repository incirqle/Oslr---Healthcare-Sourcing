Questions about pricing.

Credits are used when you reveal a contact detail, such as a work email, personal email or phone number. You are only charged when a detail is found. Searching and reviewing results do not use credits, and a revealed detail is saved to Contacts so you never pay for it twice.

Credits reset at the start of each billing period. Enterprise plans can set different terms.

Annual billing takes 15% off. Starter is $1,316 a year and Pro is $2,336 a year, paid up front.

Yes. Move between plans or billing periods by emailing hello@oslr.health. If you cancel, your plan stays active through the end of the billing period.

Oslr indexes publicly available professional information about U.S. healthcare professionals through licensed data partners. It does not handle patient data. See Security and data.

Oslr is built not to handle Protected Health Information, so it is not a HIPAA business associate and does not sign BAAs. A clinician's specialty, training and work contact details are not PHI.
