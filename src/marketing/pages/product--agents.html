Questions

How is an agent different from a search?

A search answers a question once. An agent keeps working on it every day, learns from each person you approve or pass, and brings only new people each morning.

How many people will it bring me?

Up to 10 a day, and never anyone you have already reviewed. You can set it lower when you create the agent.

Does an agent contact clinicians?

No. Agents find and rank people. You decide who to reach out to and look up contact details yourself, the same way you do in search.

Can I change what it looks for?

Yes. Steer it in the chat at any time, add notes when you decide, or skip a town or employer. Changes apply from the next batch.
