What moves into your ATS

Every candidate you send carries the details that matter in a clinical hire, in fields your team can filter and report on.

  • Name and current title
  • Specialty and subspecialty
  • Training stage, such as PGY-5 or current fellow
  • Current employer and practice setting
  • City, metro and state
  • LinkedIn profile
  • Work email, personal email and phone you revealed
  • The Oslr summary for each candidate
  • Project name and candidate status

Questions

Which plans include integrations?

ATS and CRM integrations are included on Pro and Enterprise. Starter includes CSV export, which you can import into any system.

What gets sent to my ATS or CRM?

The candidate details you see in Oslr, the contact details you have revealed, and the project and status you set. You choose which candidates to send.

Does Oslr send candidates automatically?

No. Nothing leaves Oslr until you send it, one candidate at a time or a whole project at once.

Can we map Oslr fields to our own custom fields?

Yes, on Enterprise. We set up the mapping with your team so specialty, training stage and status land in the fields you already use.

My system is not on the list. Can we still connect?

Tell us which one you use. We are adding systems based on what our customers run, and CSV export works with everything in the meantime.

Does Oslr handle patient data?

No. Oslr works with professional profile and contact data for clinicians only.
