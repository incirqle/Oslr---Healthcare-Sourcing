How Oslr reads a search.

Tap any part of the search to see what Oslr checks.

Find residents at with an

Training yearPGY-5

Oslr reads when residency started, so the person is in their fifth year today. A resident title alone is not enough.

Specialtygeneral surgery

The specialty of the residency program, kept separate from surgical subspecialties and fellowships.

EmployerMass General Brigham

The health system and its affiliated hospitals. Add a hospital name to narrow to one site.

Locationnear Boston

The Boston metro area, not only the city limits.

Interestinterest in trauma

Read from About sections, training and previous roles, so a resident drawn to trauma ranks higher.

Current fellowFellowship has started and has not ended yet.
Interventional cardiologyThe subspecialty itself, kept separate from general cardiology and electrophysiology.
Private practiceIndependent and private groups, not health-system employed physicians.
CRNA, SRNAPracticing nurse anesthetists and students in nurse anesthesia programs, kept apart.
BSN, CCRNDegrees and certifications listed on the profile.
the SouthwestArizona, New Mexico, Nevada, Utah and West Texas, mapped for you.
