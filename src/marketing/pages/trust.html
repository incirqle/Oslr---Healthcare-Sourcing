Data handling

Oslr works with professional information about clinicians and the account data you create. Nothing clinical, nothing about patients.

  • No patient data or PHI, by design
  • No connections to EHR or EMR systems
  • Professional information only: name, role, specialty, training, employer, location and work contact details
  • Oslr does not search or sort by race, religion, health or other sensitive categories
  • Customers agree not to upload PHI

Infrastructure security

Oslr runs on established cloud providers with security built into every layer.

  • Encryption in transit with current TLS
  • Encryption at rest for databases and backups
  • Customer data separated at the application and database layers
  • Encrypted backups kept for disaster recovery

Access control

Only the people who need access have it, and every sensitive system sits behind strong sign-in.

  • Multi-factor authentication on sensitive systems
  • Least-privilege access for the Oslr team
  • Production access limited and reviewed
  • Unique accounts for every person, no shared logins

Secure development

Every change to Oslr is reviewed before it ships.

  • Code review on every change
  • Dependency scanning for known vulnerabilities
  • Secrets scanning so keys never land in code
  • Separate development and production environments

Responsible AI

Oslr uses AI to read your search and describe the people it finds. People make every hiring decision.

  • Oslr surfaces candidates. It does not make or score hiring decisions
  • Your searches and projects are not used to train AI models
  • AI providers process requests under agreements that bar training on your data
  • Every summary is based on the professional profile shown beside it

Privacy and data rights

Every clinician in Oslr can see, correct or remove their information, and we act on requests quickly.

  • GDPR, UK GDPR and CCPA rights to access, correct and delete, honored for every clinician
  • Removed profiles stay out of future results
  • Requests answered within the timelines privacy law requires
  • A Data Processing Agreement for every customer who needs one

Vendor management

We choose a small number of trusted providers and hold them to the same standard we hold ourselves.

  • Data processing agreements with providers that handle personal information
  • Providers reviewed for security before we use them
  • Professional data sourced only from licensed data partners and public professional profiles

Incident response

A documented plan so that if something happens, you hear from us fast and with the facts.

  • Documented incident response process
  • Prompt notification to affected customers, in line with legal requirements
  • Root cause review after every incident

Is Oslr GDPR compliant?

Yes. Oslr is built to meet GDPR and UK GDPR requirements, with a lawful basis for processing, rights requests honored for every clinician, and a Data Processing Agreement for customers.

Does Oslr follow CCPA and U.S. state privacy laws?

Yes. Clinicians can ask to know, correct or delete their information, or opt out, and we honor those requests within the timelines the law sets.

Does Oslr handle patient data or PHI?

No. Oslr works only with professional information about clinicians. It never connects to an EHR and never touches patient records, so it is not a HIPAA business associate.

Does Oslr have a SOC 2 report?

Not today. Our controls are documented, and we are happy to complete your security questionnaire and walk your team through our architecture under NDA.

Is my data used to train AI models?

No. Your searches, projects and contacts are used to run Oslr for your team and nothing else.

Who are Oslr's service providers?

A small set of established providers for hosting, database, AI processing, email and payments. The full list with roles and regions is available to customers on request.

Where does Oslr's clinician data come from?

Licensed data partners and public professional profiles. Oslr holds professional information only, never anything clinical.
