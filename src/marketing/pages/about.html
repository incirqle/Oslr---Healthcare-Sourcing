Meet OslrHow it works

WHY WE BUILT IT

Clinical talent is not
a keyword problem.

Healthcare recruiting has its own language. A resident is not an attending. A specialty is not a subspecialty. A training year can change who is right for an opening.

We built Oslr because the people filling those roles deserve more than a general-purpose search tool. They deserve technology that understands clinical terminology, training pathways and practice settings.

Our goal is to level the playing field for healthcare organizations, from an independent practice to a health system, by making clinical talent easier to find and understand.

THE TEAM BEHIND OSLR

Clinical perspective.
Entrepreneurial drive.
AI expertise.

Oslr brings together clinicians, entrepreneurs and AI specialists around one shared focus: building a better way to connect healthcare organizations with clinical talent.

Clinical insight grounds the work in the realities of healthcare. Entrepreneurial thinking keeps it focused on real problems. AI expertise turns that understanding into a tool that works with the language people already use.

Purpose-built is not just how we describe Oslr. It is how we approach the work.

WHAT GUIDES US

Healthcare first. People always.

Clinical depth

Specialties, subspecialties, training stages and practice settings matter. We build for those distinctions.

Human judgment

Technology helps surface possibilities. People make the decisions about who belongs on their team.

Responsible data

Professional clinician information, not patient records. Respect for privacy belongs at the center of the product.
