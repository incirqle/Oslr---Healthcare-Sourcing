Who we are

Oslr is a clinical talent search service operated by Pitch Sink Inc. ("Oslr", "we", "us"). Oslr helps healthcare recruiters, health systems, surgery centers and private practices find and organize clinicians for open roles. This policy covers oslr.health and the Oslr application.

For our customers' workspaces, Oslr acts as a processor (service provider) for the data customers add. For the professional clinician information Oslr provides in search, Oslr acts as a controller.

Information we collect

From customers and users

  • Account details: name, work email, company name, role and password, or your Google account name, email and profile picture when you sign in with Google.
  • Workspace content: searches, projects, saved candidates, notes, statuses, agent briefs, review decisions and contacts you reveal.
  • Billing details: plan, invoices and payment status. Card details are handled by Stripe and never stored by Oslr.
  • Usage and device data: pages used, actions taken, search counts, IP address, browser type and timestamps, used for security, metering and fraud prevention.
  • Communications: messages you send us and feedback you submit.

Information we never collect

Oslr does not collect, store or process patient data or Protected Health Information (PHI). Oslr does not connect to EHR or EMR systems. Customers agree not to upload PHI. Oslr does not search, sort or infer race, ethnicity, religion, health, sexual orientation or other sensitive categories.

Clinician professional information

Oslr shows professional information about U.S. healthcare professionals so recruiters can contact them about job opportunities. This includes name, title, specialty and subspecialty, training stage, education, employer and practice affiliations, work history, professional location, public registry identifiers such as the NPI, publicly reported research and clinical trial participation, publicly reported industry payments, and business or professional contact details.

This information comes from licensed professional data providers, public professional profiles and public government registries such as the CMS NPI Registry, CMS Open Payments, PubMed and ClinicalTrials.gov. If you are a clinician, you can ask to see, correct or remove your information at any time (see Your rights). Removed profiles stay out of future results.

How we use information

  • To provide, run and improve Oslr, including search, ranking, agents, projects and contact reveals.
  • To meter usage, apply plan limits and credits, and bill subscriptions.
  • To secure the service, detect automated or abusive use, and prevent fraud.
  • To send service emails such as sign-in links, password resets, receipts and billing notices.
  • To deliver exports and integrations you turn on, such as Google Sheets, Excel or Slack.
  • To meet legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

Your searches, projects and contacts are not used to train AI models. AI providers process requests under agreements that bar training on your data. Oslr surfaces candidates; people make every hiring decision.

Google user data

When you sign in with Google, Oslr receives your name, email address and profile picture to create and secure your account. When you connect Google Sheets or Google Drive, Oslr requests access only to create and update the spreadsheets Oslr makes for you, so it can keep your project and agent exports up to date. Oslr does not read, change or delete other files in your Google account.

Oslr's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Oslr does not sell Google user data, does not use it for advertising, does not use it to train AI models, and does not let people read it except with your consent, for security, or where the law requires. You can disconnect Google at any time in Oslr Settings or in your Google account permissions.

Legal bases (GDPR and UK GDPR)

  • Contract: to provide Oslr to customers and their users.
  • Legitimate interests: to show professional clinician information for business-to-business recruiting, to secure the service and to improve it. We balance these interests against the rights of the people involved and honor objections.
  • Consent: for optional integrations you connect, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Legal obligation: for tax, accounting and lawful requests.

How we share information

Oslr does not sell personal information for money. We share information only with:

  • Service providers that host, run and support Oslr under written agreements. See the Subprocessors list.
  • Your workspace: members of your team can see shared projects, candidates and contacts.
  • Destinations you choose: such as your Google Sheet, Excel file, Slack channel or ATS.
  • Legal and safety: when required by law, or to protect rights, safety and the service.
  • Business transfers: in a merger, acquisition or asset sale, under this policy's protections.

Retention

Account and workspace data is kept while your account is active and deleted or anonymized within 90 days after closure, except where the law requires longer, such as billing records. Search results are cached for up to 90 days and clinician research data for up to 12 months to avoid repeat lookups. Security logs are kept for up to 12 months.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the use of your information, receive a copy in a portable format, and withdraw consent. Clinicians can ask us to remove their profile from Oslr. Email hello@oslr.health with the subject "Privacy request". We verify requests and answer within the time the law sets, usually 30 days for GDPR and 45 days for CCPA. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

California and U.S. state rights

Residents of California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Texas, Utah, Oregon and other states with privacy laws have the right to know, access, correct and delete personal information, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, targeted advertising and profiling. Oslr does not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. To make a request, including a "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" request, email hello@oslr.health. An authorized agent may request on your behalf. We will not discriminate against you for using these rights. We honor Global Privacy Control signals.

Security

Oslr encrypts data in transit and at rest, separates each customer's data, limits staff access and reviews every change before it ships. See the Trust Center. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will notify you as the law requires.

International transfers

Oslr is based in the United States and processes data in the United States and the European Union. Transfers from the EEA, UK and Switzerland rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or another lawful mechanism.

Children

Oslr is a business service and is not meant for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes and contact

We will post changes here and update the effective date. For material changes, we will notify account owners by email. Questions: Pitch Sink Inc., attention Oslr Privacy, hello@oslr.health.
