Six ways to narrow a search.

Combine any of them in one sentence, the way you would brief a colleague.

Specialty and subspecialty

Interventional cardiology, pediatric anesthesia, Mohs surgery. Oslr separates the subspecialty from the broader field.

Training stage

PGY year, current fellow, chief resident or attending, read from when training actually started.

Practice setting

Private practice, hospital, academic center or surgery center, so you reach the setting you are hiring into.

Location

City, metro, state or region. Ask for “the Southwest” or “within reach of Boston” and Oslr maps it.

Employer

Search inside one health system, a group of practices, or everyone who has ever worked at a hospital.

Interest and background

Read from About sections, residency training and previous roles, so a former nurse or therapist stands out.
