Outreach to physicians

A four-email sequence for board-certified attendings, with cadence and fill-in fields.

On this page

  2. Email 1: Introduction (Day 0)
  3. Email 2: Follow-up (Day 4)

  5. Email 4: Close (Day 21)
  6. Before you send

A four-email sequence over 21 days for board-certified attendings. Direct, peer-to-peer and respectful of a busy schedule.

Best for: subspecialty searches, mid-career and senior attending recruiting, hospital-employed and private-practice roles. Cadence: 4 emails over 21 days. Typical reply rate: 8 to 15 percent, depending on specialty and personalization.

Cadence

Email Day Purpose
1 Day 0 Introduction: who you are, why you are reaching out, the role in one sentence
2 Day 4 Short follow-up that adds one new detail
3 Day 11 What would be different about this role for them
4 Day 21 Polite close that leaves the door open

Stop on any reply. If Email 3 was opened several times, wait another week before the close.

Email 1: Introduction (Day 0)

Subject: [first_name], [specialty] role with [practice_name] in [role_city]

Hi [first_name], I'm [your_first_name], recruiting for [practice_name] in [role_city]. I came across your profile through [specific_connection: fellowship, current employer, training program] and wanted to reach out directly. We have an opening for a [specialty] [role_title] starting [start_window]. The role is [practice_setting: academic, community, private practice or hospital-employed]. Compensation is competitive for the market, and we are flexible on start date for the right person. If you would consider it, even just to learn more, I would value a call. If now is not the right time, that is fine. I will keep my notes short. Thanks, [your_first_name] [your_title], [practice_name] [your_phone]

Email 2: Follow-up (Day 4)

Subject: Re: [first_name], [specialty] role with [practice_name] in [role_city]

Hi [first_name], Following up on my note from earlier this week. Happy to keep this by email if that is easier. One thing I should have mentioned: [one_specific_detail: case mix, equipment, partnership track, schedule, geography]. It is relevant to anyone with your training. If you would like to learn more, here is my calendar: [calendar_link]. Thanks, [your_first_name]

Email 3: Specific value (Day 11)

Subject: [first_name], one more detail on the [specialty] role

Hi [first_name], I won't keep writing if this is not a fit, but one more detail may change the math. [specific_value_paragraph] If any of that is interesting, I would like to set up a call. If not, I will send one more note and stop. [your_first_name]

Pick one value paragraph and adapt it:

  • Compensation. "Compensation is structured as [base plus production, salary, or partnership track]. For someone with your fellowship training I would expect first-year total compensation in the [range], with a clear path to [partner or leadership] within [N] years."
  • Practice. "Our group sees [N] cases a month in your subspecialty, with [equipment, volume or case mix]. The team includes [N] other [subspecialty] physicians, so call is [frequency]."
  • Place. "[role_city] offers [schools, partner job market, family ties, cost of living]."
  • Schedule. "We built this role around [protected research time, a clinical-only schedule, a four-day week, no inpatient call], which is unusual for the specialty in this region."

Email 4: Close (Day 21)

Subject: Closing the loop, [first_name]

Hi [first_name], This is my last note. I know how often you are contacted. If your timing changes, or a colleague might be a fit for [practice_name], you have my email and number. Wishing you well. [your_first_name] [your_title], [practice_name]

Before you send

  • The connection in Email 1 has to be real. "I came across your profile because [actual detail]" beats a generic line.
  • The detail in Email 2 has to be specific. If you cannot name one, the role is not ready to recruit on.
  • Email 3 must be true. If you cannot say it plainly, skip it and send Email 4.
  • Keep subject lines plain. Direct subject lines do better with physicians.
  • Add a postal address to your footer. CAN-SPAM requires it.
