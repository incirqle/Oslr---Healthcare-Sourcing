Outreach to NPs and PAs

A four-email sequence for advanced practice providers, with room for state scope.

On this page

  1. Cadence
  2. Email 1: Introduction (Day 0)
  3. Email 2: Follow-up (Day 4)
  4. Email 3: The day to day (Day 11)
  5. Email 4: Close (Day 21)
  6. Before you send

A four-email sequence over 21 days for nurse practitioners and physician assistants. Slightly less formal than the physician sequence, with room for state scope of practice.

Best for: primary care, urgent care, surgical specialty, hospital medicine and specialty clinic roles. Cadence: 4 emails over 21 days. Typical reply rate: 10 to 18 percent, depending on specialty and location.

Cadence

Email Day Purpose
1 Day 0 Introduction: who you are, the role, the setting
2 Day 4 One specific detail
3 Day 11 Autonomy, schedule and pay
4 Day 21 Polite close

Stop on any reply.

Email 1: Introduction (Day 0)

Subject: [first_name], [role_type] [NP or PA] role at [practice_name] in [role_city]

Hi [first_name], I'm [your_first_name] with [practice_name] in [role_city]. I came across your profile and wanted to reach out about a [role_type] [NP or PA] position. The role is [practice_setting: primary care, urgent care, surgical specialty, hospital medicine, outpatient clinic]. The schedule is [schedule: Monday to Friday with no weekends, or 3x12s]. We are [physician_model: physician-collaborative with a clear scope, or independent within state scope]. If you would like to learn more, I would value a call, and I am happy to share details by email first. Thanks, [your_first_name] [your_title], [practice_name] [your_phone]

Email 2: Follow-up (Day 4)

Subject: Re: [first_name], [role_type] role at [practice_name]

Hi [first_name], Following up on my note from earlier this week. One thing I should have mentioned: [one_specific_detail: patient volume, team makeup, ramp, supervising physician model, charting time, time off]. It tends to matter to APPs weighing [role_type] roles. If you are open to it, here is my calendar: [calendar_link]. Thanks, [your_first_name]

Email 3: The day to day (Day 11)

Subject: [first_name], what the day to day looks like

Hi [first_name], I won't keep writing if this is not a fit, but here is a clearer picture of the role: Patients per day: [realistic_number] Schedule: [shift or clinic pattern] Scope: [how independent, within state scope] Compensation: [base plus bonus, or rate] Flexibility: [any flexibility offered] If that fits what you want, here is my calendar: [calendar_link]. If not, I will send one more note and stop. [your_first_name]

Email 4: Close (Day 21)

Subject: Closing the loop, [first_name]

Hi [first_name], This is my last note. If your timing changes, or a colleague comes to mind, just reply here. Wishing you well. [your_first_name] [your_title], [practice_name]

Before you send

  • Match the credential exactly. A PA addressed as an NP, or the reverse, reads as careless.
  • Name state scope where it matters. If the role is in a full-practice-authority state, say so.
  • Lead with schedule when it stands out. For many APPs, schedule and volume beat salary.
  • Run NP and PA searches separately. Different credentials, training and state rules.
