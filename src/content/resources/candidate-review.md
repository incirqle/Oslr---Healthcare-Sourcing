# Reviewing a clinician

Click any clinician's name to open their profile. This is where you decide whether they fit.

---

## The profile panel

- **Header**: name, current title, employer and location, plus the match badge.
- **Experience**: every job with the employer's logo, title and dates.
- **Education and training**: schools, residency and fellowship with logos.
- **Match criteria**: your criteria with **Yes / Partly / Not shown / No**, and the line from their profile that backs each answer. Click an answer to jump to that line.
- **Contact** tab: the mint **Find contact info** button. See [Contact info](/resources/contacts).

## Review mode

Review mode shows one clinician at a time, full screen, so you can move fast. The criteria panel starts collapsed with a summary (for example *4 yes, 0 partly*); open it or use **expand all**. Use the arrow keys to move between clinicians.

## Research & Industry (doctors)

For physicians, the **Research & Industry** tab shows public information:

- **Industry payments** reported to the federal Open Payments database, with the companies' logos and a chart.
- **Publications** from PubMed, matched carefully to the right doctor.
- **Clinical trials** they are listed on.

This is free and saved for a year, so it opens instantly next time.

## Company insights

Click an employer to open its company panel. Everything here is free to view.

- **Overview**: what the organization is, founded year, size, locations and leadership.
- **People**: the clinical role mix, departments, training pipeline and where staff are located.
- **Movement**: a talent flow showing where people came from and where they went, with each employer's logo, plus joined, left and net change.

Individual names behind these numbers are only pulled when you choose to see them. Company data is saved for 90 days and shared by your team.
