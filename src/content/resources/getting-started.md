# Your first 10 minutes in Oslr

Oslr finds doctors, nurses and allied health clinicians for the roles you are hiring. You describe who you need in plain English, Oslr returns matching clinicians, and you save the right ones into a project. This guide gets you from sign-in to your first saved clinician.

---

## 1. Sign in

Go to **oslr.health** and click **Get started** (new) or **Sign in** (returning). You can use email and password or **Continue with Google**. If a teammate invited you, open the link in their email first; it drops you straight into their workspace.

## 2. Land on the Dashboard

![The Dashboard](/resources/dashboard.png)

The Dashboard shows saved clinicians, active projects, searches today and the credits left in your shared pool. Every card is clickable. See [Dashboard](/resources/dashboard).

## 3. Make a project for the role

Click **Projects** in the left sidebar, then **New project**. Name it after the role, for example *Southwest pain physicians*. Everything for that role, its searches, saved clinicians, agents and live sync, lives here. See [Projects](/resources/projects).

## 4. Run a search

Inside the project click **Talent Search** (top tab) or type straight into the box that says *Describe who you need for this role*. Try:

> Interventional pain physicians in Phoenix, Arizona

Press Enter. Results come back ranked, with a match score for each person.

## 5. Review and save

Click a name to open the full profile: experience, education, match criteria and company insights. Click **Save** to add them to the project. See [Reviewing a clinician](/resources/candidate-review).

## 6. Get contact info (only when you want it)

Searching, reading profiles and company insights are free. Credits are used only when you click **Find contact info** and Oslr actually finds an email or phone. If nothing is found, you are not charged. See [Credits](/resources/credits).

---

## What to try next

- Turn the search into an **Agent** that keeps finding new matches every day. See [Oslr Agents](/resources/agents).
- Send saved clinicians to **Google Sheets, Slack or Excel** automatically. See [Live sync](/resources/live-sync).
- **Invite your team** so everyone shares the same projects and credits. See [Inviting your team](/resources/team-and-seats).
