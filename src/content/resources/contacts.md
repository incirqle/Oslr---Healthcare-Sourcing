# Contact info and Contacts

## Finding a clinician's contact info

Open a clinician and click the mint **Find contact info** button (also on the **Contact** tab). Choose what you want:

| Type | Starter | Pro |
|---|---|---|
| Work email | 2 credits | 1 credit |
| Personal email | 3 credits | 2 credits |
| Phone | 3 credits | 3 credits |

How it works:

- Oslr checks several trusted sources in order and keeps the freshest, most deliverable result for each type.
- **You are only charged for what is found.** If nothing is found you see *Nothing was found. You will not be charged.*
- **You never pay twice.** Once anyone on your team unlocks a clinician's info, it is saved for the whole workspace.
- From a project you can select several clinicians and find contact info in bulk.

If you have a monthly spending limit and reach it, new lookups pause until the 1st of next month or until an admin raises it. See [Credits](/resources/credits).

## The Contacts page

![Contacts](/resources/contacts.png)

**Contacts** in the sidebar lists every clinician your team has saved across all projects. Search by name, filter by project or status, and open anyone to see their profile and any contact info already unlocked. From a project, **View in Contacts** opens this page filtered to that project.
