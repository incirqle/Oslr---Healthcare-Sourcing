# Dashboard

The Dashboard is the first screen after you sign in. It answers "where do things stand today?"

![The Dashboard](/resources/dashboard.png)

---

## The four numbers

| Card | Shows | Click to go to |
|---|---|---|
| Saved Clinicians | Everyone your team has saved, plus how many this week | Contacts |
| Active Projects | Projects in your workspace, plus how many moved this week | Projects |
| Searches Today | Searches run today against your daily allowance | Settings > Usage & activity |
| Available Credits | Credits left in your shared monthly pool | Settings > Billing & invoices |

## Agents

If any agent has clinicians waiting for you, the Dashboard shows it so you can jump straight into review. Agents that are still working show as **Working**.

## Getting back here

Click **Dashboard** in the sidebar or the Oslr logo at the top left.
