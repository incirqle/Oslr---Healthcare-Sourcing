# Credits and spending limits

Credits are only for things that cost us money to fetch. Most of Oslr is free.

![Shared credits & limits](/resources/settings-credits.png)

---

## Free

- Searching, including reopening saved searches
- Reading profiles, match criteria and ranking changes
- Research & Industry (payments, publications, trials)
- Company insights
- Agents' daily searching
- Live sync and integrations

## Uses credits

| Action | Starter | Pro |
|---|---|---|
| Work email found | 2 | 1 |
| Personal email found | 3 | 2 |
| Phone found | 3 | 3 |
| Starting an agent | 15 | 15 |

Contact info is charged only when something is found, and never twice for the same clinician in your workspace.

## One shared pool

Credits belong to the workspace, not each person. Starter gets 150 a month; Pro gets 500 for every seat in use.

## Monthly reset

Credits refresh on your billing date each month. Unused credits do not roll over. Yearly plans still receive credits one month at a time.

## Member spending limits (admins)

In **Shared credits & limits**, set a monthly limit for any member. When they reach it, their new lookups pause until the 1st of the next month (UTC) or until you raise it. A limit of 0 pauses their spending entirely. Leave it empty for no limit.

## Where to see your balance

Dashboard > **Available Credits**, or this Settings section.
