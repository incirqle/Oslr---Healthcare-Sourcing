# Inviting your team

Everyone in a workspace shares the same projects, saved clinicians, unlocked contact info and credit pool.

![Team & seats](/resources/settings-team.png)

---

## Seats by plan

| Plan | Seats | Shared credits per month |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | 1 | 150 |
| Pro | Up to 5 | 500 for each seat in use (up to 2,500) |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom |

On Pro, the pool grows by 500 the moment a new teammate joins.

## Inviting someone (admins)

1. Settings > **Team & seats** > **Invite**.
2. Enter their work email.
3. Pick a role:
   - **Admin**: everything, including billing and invitations
   - **Recruiter**: search, save, find contact info, run agents
   - **Viewer**: read-only
4. Optionally set a monthly credit limit for them.
5. Send. They get an email from Oslr with a join link. You can also **Copy link** to send it yourself.

A pending invitation holds a seat until it is accepted or cancelled. Use **Resend** or **Cancel** on the pending list.

The link only works for the email it was sent to. Someone who already belongs to another active workspace cannot be moved by an invite; contact us to merge.

## If you are out of seats

Starter has one seat. Upgrade to Pro to add teammates: Settings > Billing & invoices > **Upgrade to Pro**, or **Review plan** on the Team page.

## Changing roles or removing someone

On the member's row, change the role or remove them. Their saved work stays in the workspace.
