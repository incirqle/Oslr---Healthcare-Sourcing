# Live sync: Google Sheets, Slack and Excel

Live sync sends a project's clinicians to where your team already works, within seconds of an approval or a contact being found. Available on every plan.

![Connections & Sync](/resources/project-connections.png)

---

## Where to set it up

Open the project and click **Connections & Sync** (top tab or sidebar), or click **Live sync** in the project's Connections box. Agents also let you pick a destination when you start them.

## Google Sheets

1. Click **Connect** on Google Sheets and sign in with your Google account.
2. Oslr creates a sheet for the project (it can only see sheets it creates).
3. New and updated clinicians appear as rows. Updates change the same row; Oslr never wipes your sheet.

Click **Open** to open the sheet, **Change** to pick another.

## Slack

1. Click **Connect** on Slack and paste the channel's incoming webhook link.
2. Oslr posts a test message first so you can see it worked.
3. Each approval posts a message. **Send now** pushes the current list.

## Excel

Excel uses a private link. Click **Copy link** and add it in Excel through *Data > From Web*. Excel refreshes from the link. If the link is ever shared by mistake, click the circular-arrow button next to it to make a new one and the old one stops working.
