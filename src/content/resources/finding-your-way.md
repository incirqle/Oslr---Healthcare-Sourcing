# Finding your way around

Oslr always tells you three things: which project you are in, which part of it you are looking at, and how to get back.

---

## The left sidebar

**Workspace** (always there):

| Item | What it is |
|---|---|
| Dashboard | Today's numbers and quick links |
| Projects | Every role you are hiring for |
| Talent Search | Search for clinicians |
| All agents | Every agent across all projects |
| Contacts | Every clinician your team has saved |
| Integrations | ATS, CRM and sync connections |
| News | Healthcare hiring news |

**Settings**: Resources (this help center) and Settings (team, credits, billing).

## When you are inside a project

![Inside a project](/resources/project-detail.png)

The sidebar adds a **Current project** block at the top with the project name and its four parts:

- **Candidates**: saved clinicians and the project's search history
- **Talent Search**: searches that save straight into this project
- **Project agents**: agents working this role
- **Connections & Sync**: where approved clinicians are sent

The same four parts appear as tabs under the project name at the top of the page.

## The path at the top

The top bar shows your path, for example **Projects > Southwest pain physicians > Agents > Southwest pain physicians**. Click any piece to jump back to it. Every project page also has **Back to Projects**.

## Agents always show their project

An agent page always says which project it belongs to and shows *Approved clinicians save to [project]*, so you know where your approvals go.

## Quick "where do I go" list

| I want to... | Go to |
|---|---|
| Start a new role | Projects > New project |
| Re-run an old search | Open the project > Candidates > Searches list > the circular arrow |
| See everyone we saved | Contacts |
| Check credits left | Dashboard, or Settings > Shared credits & limits |
| Download an invoice | Settings > Billing & invoices |
| Add a teammate | Settings > Team & seats |
| Connect Google Sheets or Slack | Open the project > Connections & Sync |
| Connect an ATS or CRM | Integrations |
