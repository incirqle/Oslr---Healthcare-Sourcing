# ATS and CRM integrations

Push clinicians straight into your applicant tracking system or CRM. Available on **Pro** and **Enterprise**. Starter teams can use [Live sync](/resources/live-sync) to Sheets, Slack and Excel.

![Integrations](/resources/integrations.png)

---

## Connecting

1. Click **Integrations** in the sidebar (or Settings > Connections).
2. Search for your system, for example Workday, Taleo, UKG Pro, SmartRecruiters or Lever.
3. Click **Connect** and sign in to your system in the window that opens. An admin of your system may need to approve it.
4. When it says **Connected**, you are done.

Your system's login is stored securely on our side and is never shown in the browser.

## Sending clinicians

- From a project: use the export menu and choose your connected system.
- From an agent: choose the system as a destination so approvals go there automatically.

Systems marked **Coming soon** are not available yet.
