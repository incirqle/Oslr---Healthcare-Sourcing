# Questions and fixes

**I can't find a search I ran.**
Open the project it belongs to > Candidates > Searches. Searches run without a project, or before searches were tied to projects, do not appear there.

**A search returned nothing useful.**
Be more specific about role and place, or loosen one thing (a wider area, fewer requirements). Oslr covers clinical roles only. See [Writing good searches](/resources/best-practices/writing-good-searches).

**Was I charged for a lookup that found nothing?**
No. You are only charged when contact info is found.

**My lookups stopped working.**
You may have reached your monthly spending limit, or the shared pool is empty. Check Settings > Shared credits & limits, or ask an admin.

**I can't invite a teammate.**
Only admins can invite, and Starter has one seat. Upgrade to Pro for up to 5 seats.

**I don't see Integrations working.**
ATS and CRM connections need Enterprise. Sheets, Slack and Excel work on every plan.

**My agent isn't finding new people.**
Review its waiting matches first; the next batch starts after you review. Check it isn't paused and that you haven't hit the daily match limit.

**I didn't get the invite or reset email.**
Check spam for mail from notify.oslr.health, then ask for it to be resent.

**Where are my invoices?**
Settings > Billing & invoices (admins only).
