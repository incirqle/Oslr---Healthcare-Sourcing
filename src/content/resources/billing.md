Prices shown in Oslr come straight from our billing system.

## Subscribing

After signing up you see **Choose your plan**. Pick Starter or Pro and Monthly or Yearly, then pay in the secure form on the same screen. If you have an invite code, click **Have an invite code?**.

## Upgrading to Pro

Settings > **Billing & invoices** > **Upgrade to Pro**. You pay only the difference for the rest of the current period, and Pro features and credits switch on right away.

## Changing card, switching monthly/yearly, or cancelling

Settings > Billing & invoices. Use the **Monthly / Yearly** switch or **Manage billing** to update your card or cancel. After cancelling, you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

## Invoices

Every invoice is listed with date, amount and status. Click the download icon for the PDF. Only admins can see billing.

## A payment failed

We email the admin and retry. Update your card in Billing & invoices to avoid any interruption.
