# Settings tour

Click **Settings** at the bottom of the sidebar. The page has six sections on the left. Some are for admins only.

---

## Workspace & profile

![Workspace & profile](/resources/settings-workspace.png)

Your name and email, and your workspace (company) name. Admins can rename the workspace.

## Team & seats

![Team & seats](/resources/settings-team.png)

Members, their roles, pending invitations and seats used. Admins invite people here. See [Inviting your team](/resources/team-and-seats).

## Shared credits & limits

![Shared credits & limits](/resources/settings-credits.png)

Your shared monthly credit pool, what each contact type costs on your plan, and optional monthly spending limits per member. See [Credits](/resources/credits).

## Billing & invoices

![Billing & invoices](/resources/settings-billing.png)

Your plan, monthly or yearly billing, card on file, upgrade to Pro, and every invoice as a downloadable PDF. Admin only. See [Plans and billing](/resources/billing).

## Connections

![Connections](/resources/settings-connections.png)

ATS and CRM connections for the workspace. See [ATS and CRM](/resources/integrations).

## Usage & activity

![Usage & activity](/resources/settings-usage.png)

Searches today, credits spent and recent activity across the team.
