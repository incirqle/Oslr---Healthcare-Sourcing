# Oslr Agents

An agent is a sourcer that works one role for you. Describe who you want once; the agent searches on a schedule, brings you new matches to approve or pass, and learns from every decision.

![An agent at work](/resources/agent-detail.png)

---

## Starting an agent

1. Open a project. In the box *Describe who you need for this role*, switch from **Search** to **Agent**.
2. Describe the role, for example *Interventional pain physicians in Arizona, fellowship trained*. Oslr shows how it understood your description before anything runs.
3. Pick where approved clinicians should go (the project, plus Sheets, Slack or Excel if you like). You can change this later.
4. Start it. Starting an agent uses **15 credits** from your shared pool.

You can also go to **All agents** in the sidebar.

## Plan limits

| Plan | Active agents | New matches per day |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | 1 | 10 |
| Pro | 4 per teammate | 25 |
| Enterprise | Unlimited | Custom |

Paused or finished agents do not count toward the limit.

## Reviewing matches

- **To review**, **Approved** and **Passed** tabs list the agent's clinicians.
- Each card shows photo, title, employer, location, a short summary and why they matched. Click it to expand experience and education.
- Thumbs up approves, thumbs down passes. Keyboard: **A** to approve, **P** to pass. Add a reason when passing; the agent uses it.
- Approved clinicians save to the project shown at the top (*Approved clinicians save to ...*) and go to any connected sync.

## How it learns

The agent starts in **Learning your taste**. Review your first matches and the next batch adapts. The right panel shows **Today** (your decisions, review list, next search), **Looks for** (what it has learned you want), **Skips** (what it avoids) and **Timeline**.

## Talk to your agent

Open **Talk to your agent** to change it in plain English: *only board-eligible fellows*, *skip anyone at large hospital systems*, *pause until Monday*. The agent confirms what it changed.

## Cost

Starting an agent is 15 credits. Daily searching is free. Contact info is charged only when you request it and something is found.
