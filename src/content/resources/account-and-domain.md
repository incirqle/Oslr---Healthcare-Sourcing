# Sign-in, emails and domain

## Where Oslr lives

The app and website are at **oslr.health**. Bookmark **oslr.health/dashboard** to go straight in.

## Signing in

- **Email and password**: the password bar shows strength as you type.
- **Continue with Google**: use your work Google account.
- **Forgot password**: on the sign-in page click **Forgot password?**. We email a reset link; it opens a page to set a new password.

## Emails from Oslr

All our emails come from **notify.oslr.health**:

- Confirm your email (sign-up)
- Reset your password
- Team invitations
- Billing notices (receipts, failed payments)

Not seeing one? Check spam or promotions and add notify.oslr.health to your safe senders. Your IT team can allow it too.

## Your workspace

Your workspace is your company inside Oslr. Its name shows at the top of Settings. Everyone you invite joins it.

## Privacy and terms

Read them at **oslr.health/privacy** and **oslr.health/terms**.

## Signing out

Click the arrow next to your email at the bottom of the sidebar.
