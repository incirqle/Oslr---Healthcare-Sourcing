- Resident-year asks (PGY years, fellowship not asked) are decided in `resident-year.ts`: asked-year current residents only (written PGY/chief first, then start dates allowing research years); fellows and other years removed, undated set aside; generic resident titles need specialty evidence, and `market-fit.ts` drops employers known outside the searched market. Why: residents and fellows are different roles, and unclear years or hospital reputation alone must not be presented as verified specialty fit.
- City/metro discovery never requires a populated profile state; current-trainee asks use `workplace-recall.ts` for fair per-program searches without a personal-location gate, then a bounded explicit-PGY pass. `market-fit.ts` requires the actual training role at a recalled local workplace and checks current-employer headquarters, not stale home cities. Why: blank metro states and stale home cities otherwise erase qualified trainees, while one large program or secondary research role can otherwise crowd out or admit the wrong people.
