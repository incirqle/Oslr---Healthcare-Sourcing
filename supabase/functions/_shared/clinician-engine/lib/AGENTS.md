- Institutional email models are evidence interpreters, not mailbox verifiers; deterministic checks must re-validate every sample, identity, domain, pattern and collision before delivery. Why: model confidence is not measured ownership or deliverability.
- Organization-wide patterns may cross clinical roles only with unanimous verified examples from at least five people across two role groups and an explicit model decision; retain original per-role evidence and reject conflicts. Why: broad recall must not silently assume hospitals and academic affiliates share mail systems.
- Contact classification uses a server-resolved employer domain; moving an already-owned institutional address from personal to work never incurs a second charge. Why: provider buckets are not authoritative and customers must not pay twice for the same email.
