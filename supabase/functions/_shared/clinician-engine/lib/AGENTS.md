- Institutional email models are evidence interpreters, not mailbox verifiers; deterministic checks must re-validate every sample, identity, domain, pattern and collision before delivery. Why: model confidence is not measured ownership or deliverability.
- Coworkers, not company websites, decide an employer's mail domain and pattern (`institution-election.ts`): an example counts only when the address spells that coworker's own name; the domain must carry the employer's distinctive name, acronym or listed domain; at least three people, 80% agreement, unanimity under six, and the target's role group must not contradict. A listed website or link page is a hint, never a gate. Why: hospitals list link pages and careers sites, and provider 'verified' flags mark deliverability, not ownership.
- Contact classification uses a server-resolved employer domain; moving an already-owned institutional address from personal to work never incurs a second charge. Why: provider buckets are not authoritative and customers must not pay twice for the same email.
